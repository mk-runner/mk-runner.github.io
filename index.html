Biography

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate at Xidian University , advised by Prof. Qiguang Miao . My research focuses on Radiology Report Generation, Medical Vision-Language Models, and Anomaly Detection. I am passionate about advancing trustworthy and clinically applicable AI systems for medical imaging.
